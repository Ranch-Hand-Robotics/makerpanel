4 HP × 2U Maker Panel

Dimensions: 20.32 mm (width) × 88.9 mm (height)
HP Units: 4 HP (standard Eurotrack-compatible width)
Height Units: 2U (double height for more vertical space)
Max Depth: 60 mm from front surface
Material: FR4 PCB 1.6 mm thickness

Mounting

8 mounting holes (3 mm diameter) positioned for T-slot rail compatibility (2 per U)

Use Cases

  • Compact interfaces with vertical space
  • Multi-row LED displays
  • Status and control combinations
  • Stacked interfaces

Based on Maker Panel Specification v1.0
