Off-the-shelf / T-track

A continuous channel.

Conventional T-tracks use a continuous channel, typically in an aluminum extrusion. Matching T-nuts slide along the track to position your panels.

Make your own / MakerRail

Slots, not an extrusion.

MakerRail uses discrete through-slots separated by supporting ribs. 3D print the rail, or laser cut it from suitable plastic or metal sheet. No extruded channel required.

T-slot hardware compatibility does not mean identical rail profiles. Match the nuts, screws, material thickness, and clearance to your build. Compare the mounting options ↗

04 / Make room for another level

Same panels.
Another level.

MakerStack stacks MakerRail hosts vertically. Reuse ordinary flat MakerPanels at each tier: electronics below, exposed controls above, and a shared mounting interface throughout.

Supports and under-rail columns carry the stack, not the removable panels. Top-down fastener access lets you assemble and disassemble from above. Keep the driver paths clear; reaching a middle tier means removing the tiers above it first.

Explore MakerStack ↗

Choose tier spacing for your components and cables. Check clearances, driver reach, and base attachment for your build.
